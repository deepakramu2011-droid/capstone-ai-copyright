Five tools, no universal green light

Tool (Free/Basic) Contract Statement The Safety Check
ChatGPT Free User owns Output; OpenAI assigns rights it has. Outputs may not be unique; do not present AI output as human-generated.
Gemini Apps Google does not claim ownership of original content generated by some services. The standalone 2023 terms no longer apply to ordinary use; check current terms.
Claude Free Anthropic assigns any rights it has in Output to the user, subject to terms. Assignment is not uniqueness or third-party clearance; verify accuracy.
Perplexity No ownership claim; terms also grant service-use rights over content. Sources are leads, not proof; check source licences before reuse.
GitHub Copilot Free No ownership claim; output may resemble code from training data. Review, test, and check third-party licences and required notices.
Snapshot: 3 Nov 2026. Contract wording does not guarantee copyrightability or third-party clearance.
