The five-check release workflow

1. Inputs
Redact secrets/PII. Own the rights to provided prompts.
2. Terms
Check commercial use rules for your exact plan.
3. Control
Record human edits and expressive choices.
4. Similarity
Search distinctive code and text for matches.
5. Licences
Check dependencies. Keep notices. Attribute AI.
Tip: Use a dated prompt log as evidence of process.
