# CSE Capstone 55 — Copyright & Licensing Guide for AI-Generated Content and Code

This project package answers: **What can students safely do with AI-generated text, images, and code under copyright law, open licences, and AI-tool terms?** It is aimed at CSE students, designers, and open-source beginners. Research snapshot: **3 November 2026**.

## Deliverables

- `report.md` — editable, cited capstone report with legal snapshots, five-tool terms comparison, six scenarios, 10-question quiz and answer key, prompt library, verification record, and before/after analysis.
- `report/pdf/main.pdf` — polished PDF report.
- `materials/safe-use-checklist.md` — printable-style workflow checklist and attribution record.
- `materials/license-guide.md` — MIT, GPL-3.0, and Creative Commons quick guide.
- `materials/tool-terms-comparison.md` — dated five-tool matrix and safe-use conclusions.
- `materials/scenario-cards.md` — six case cards with recommended action.
- `materials/quiz.md` — 10-question quiz with answer key.
- `materials/prompt-library.md` — prompts for research, synthesis, verification, and attribution.
- `materials/verification-report.md` — source-check findings and correction log.
- `automation/scan_licenses.py` and `.github/workflows/license-audit.yml` — optional npm dependency licence screening demo.
- Companion six-slide presentation: delivered separately as a Slides resource link.

## Before submitting or presenting

1. Replace the blank name, team, course, and institution fields in the report.
2. Confirm your instructor's rules on AI assistance. Add a short firsthand test and explain what you changed or rejected.
3. Confirm the jurisdiction your instructor expects. The report compares India, the United States, and the EU; none is a substitute for local advice.
4. Re-open the linked official sources and check the exact terms for the service, plan, and date you will discuss.
5. Adapt the automation allowlist to the actual project and have a maintainer approve it. The checker flags licences for human review; it does not determine legal compatibility.
6. Disclose AI assistance in the manner your course requires. Do not describe AI-generated work as wholly human-made or attempt to evade AI-detection systems.

## Academic integrity and scope

This is an AI-assisted working draft, not evidence that a student independently authored every sentence. The source trail and verification steps are included to support learning and revision. This educational project is not legal advice.
