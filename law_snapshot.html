Three jurisdictions, careful conclusions

United States
Human Authorship

USCO's report says protection requires human expressive choices; purely AI-generated material without enough human control is not protected under its analysis.

The 2025 report says prompts alone were insufficient for the technology examined; human selection and arrangement are assessed case-by-case.

USCO Part 2 Report (2025)
India
Statutory Author

Act defines author as "the person who causes the work to be created" for computer-generated works.

Statute predates modern GenAI; application to specific prompting remains an interpretive question.

Copyright Act 1957 §2(d)(vi)
European Union
Provider Duties

AI Act imposes transparency and copyright policy duties on model providers.

GPAI obligations became applicable Aug 2025. These are provider rules, not an automatic user licence.

EU AI Act (GPAI Rules 2025)
Legal snapshots reflect official guidance as of Nov 2026. Local laws vary.
