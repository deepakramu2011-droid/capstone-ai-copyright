Four questions that must stay separate

1. Contract Ownership
What does the provider's TOS say about input and output? Many disclaim ownership or assign rights, but this is a private agreement, not a law.
2. Copyrightability
Does the work meet the human-authorship bar? In the US, purely AI-generated work without human control over expressive elements is not protected.
3. Third-Party Rights
Does the output reproduce protected material or likenesses? "Provider disclaims ownership" does not clear you of infringement risk.
4. Plan & Use Rules
Does your specific plan (Free vs Business) allow commercial use? Are there student/academic restrictions or regional rule variations?
Source: USCO Report (2025); OpenAI/Anthropic/Google Terms (Nov 2026)
