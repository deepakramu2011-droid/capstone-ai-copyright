# Five AI Tools: Terms Snapshot

**Checked:** 3 November 2026. Free/basic access, features, and terms can vary by country, account, and plan. This comparison is an educational summary, not legal advice. A provider's contract statement does not guarantee copyrightability, originality, or third-party clearance.

| Tool / basic access | What official terms say | Safe use answer |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT Free** — free access with usage limits. [1] [2] | User owns Output as between the user and OpenAI, to the extent permitted by law; OpenAI assigns any rights it has. Outputs may not be unique. Terms prohibit falsely representing output as human-generated. [1] | Use only after checking accuracy, source rights, local copyright rules, and course disclosure rules. Do not claim exclusive rights. |
| **Gemini Apps** — basic access/limits and feature availability may vary. [3] [4] | Current Google Terms say Google will not claim ownership over original content generated by some services. They prohibit using Google-service AI content to develop machine-learning models or related technology. The old standalone Generative AI Additional Terms no longer apply to ordinary use after Google's 2024 Terms update. [3] | Use current Google Terms and service notices, not the archived 2023 terms. “Google does not claim ownership” is not a licence to third-party material. |
| **Claude Free** — free tier with limited use. [5] [6] | Anthropic assigns to the user any right, title, and interest it has in Outputs, subject to compliance and applicable law. User is responsible for inputs and should independently verify outputs. [5] | The assignment does not make output unique or protectable. Do not upload material without the necessary rights. Check additional terms for evaluation features. |
| **Perplexity Standard** — free plan with basic-search limits. [7] [8] | Perplexity does not claim ownership in “Your Content,” but takes a licence to use content to operate, improve, promote, and provide the service. Output may be incorrect, similar to another user's, or raise third-party-rights issues. [7] | Treat citations as research leads. Open and verify sources; do not copy their content without checking its licence. |
| **GitHub Copilot Free** — free individual plan with limited features. [9] [10] | GitHub does not claim ownership of inputs or outputs. Output may resemble training-data code or be subject to third-party/open-source terms. The individual user is responsible for reviewing, testing, validating, and complying with licences; terms describe model-improvement use unless opted out in settings. [9] | Check distinctive suggestions, licences, notices, tests, and data-use settings before using or publishing code. Business contracts differ. |

## Commercial-use conclusion

Do not give a blanket “yes” based only on an output-ownership sentence. Check the current terms for the exact product and plan, applicable law, third-party rights, and licence obligations. If the work will be sold or used in a high-stakes product, seek qualified advice.
